📐 Position Sizing Formula Reference

Position Size = MIN[(Portfolio × Risk%) / Stop%, Portfolio / Max Positions]

Risk-Based Position Sizing:

Position size is driven by your risk tolerance, with a hard cap to prevent over-concentration. The 1/N diversification slot is shown as a reference — a warning appears when the risk-based size exceeds it, but it no longer caps your shares.

Primary: (Portfolio × Risk%) / Stop% — size based on your chosen risk %

Hard cap: 25% of account — no single position may exceed this, regardless of risk slider

Diversification check: Portfolio / Max Positions — advisory 1/N slot shown for reference
